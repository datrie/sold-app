Common questions

Amazon signed me out. Sessions expire now and then. Open Sold! and tap Connect Amazon KDP to sign in again. Your history stays.

Alerts feel late. iOS decides when background apps can run, roughly every 15 to 60 minutes. Keep Background App Refresh on for Sold!, and open the app once a day so iOS keeps it high on its list. Ebook orders show up in the "orders placed" feed within minutes of the purchase; paperback orders appear when Amazon finishes processing them, which can take longer.

Do you see my password or my sales? No. You sign in on Amazon's own page inside the app, and everything stays on your phone. See the privacy policy.

I want to disconnect. Settings > Disconnect removes the session and your local history. Deleting the app does the same.
