Sold! Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 15, 2026

Sold! ("the app") is an iPhone app that shows Amazon KDP authors their own book sales. It is made by Datrie Allman ("we", "us").

The short version

Sold! does not collect, transmit, sell, or share your personal data. There is no Sold! server. Everything the app knows lives on your phone.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your Amazon session. When you sign in, you do so on Amazon's own web page inside the app. Sold! never sees or stores your Amazon password. Amazon issues session cookies to the app, and those cookies are kept on your device so the app can read your sales reports. You can remove them at any time with Settings > Disconnect, or by deleting the app.
  • Your sales figures. Sold! saves the numbers it reads from your KDP reports (orders, royalties, pages read, book titles and cover image links) on your device so it can show today's totals, detect new sales, and fill the widget.
  • Your preferences. Notification toggles and similar settings.

Where your data goes

  • To Amazon. The app talks directly to Amazon's KDP Reports website (kdpreports.amazon.com) using your session, the same way your browser does when you open the dashboard. Amazon's handling of that traffic is governed by Amazon's privacy notice.
  • Nowhere else. Sold! has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no backend of its own. We cannot see your sales, your books, or that you use the app.

Notifications

Sale alerts are generated locally on your device. No push notification service or third party is involved.

Children

Sold! is intended for adults who publish books and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If a future version adds any data collection (for example crash reporting), this policy will be updated before that version ships and the app's App Store privacy details will be updated to match.
